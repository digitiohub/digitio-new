Contrast
Introduction
Contrast is a campus-focused social connection application designed to help university students discover, connect, and communicate with relevant people within their campus network.
The application solves the problem of students finding it difficult to meet people outside their immediate classroom, course, friend circle, or existing social group. By using university email-based login, Contrast creates a more trusted campus environment where students can build profiles, express interest, match with others, chat, and participate in interest-based communities.
The platform includes a mobile application, backend system, database, and admin panel. Together, these components support secure user access, profile management, matchmaking, chat, communities, user monitoring, and overall platform control.
________________________________________
1. Problem Statement
University students often want to meet new people, build friendships, explore connections, and find communities on campus. However, these interactions are usually left to chance or happen only within existing circles.
This creates several challenges, such as:
•	Students do not easily meet people outside their course, class, or friend group
•	Starting conversations with new people can feel awkward
•	Random social media does not provide a campus-focused environment
•	Students may want a safer and more intentional way to connect
•	Campus identity matters, but most platforms are not limited to verified students
•	Students need better ways to express their personality through profiles, prompts, photos, interests, hometown, and language
•	Students may want community-based spaces, not only one-to-one matching
•	Platform activity needs to be monitored for better safety and user management
Contrast solves these problems by providing a verified campus social layer where students can discover each other, express interest, match, chat, and join communities in one place.

2. Purpose of the Application
The purpose of Contrast is to help university students break the ice and build meaningful campus connections with people they may not normally meet in daily college life.
The application allows students to create expressive profiles, add photos, answer prompts, mention interests, set preferences, discover other students, send likes, match with compatible users, and start conversations.
Contrast focuses on making student discovery more structured, intentional, and comfortable within a university-based environment.

6. How Contrast Solves the Problem
Contrast provides students with a structured and comfortable way to meet people within their university environment.
Instead of relying on random social media, chance encounters, or existing friend groups, students can discover people based on profiles, interests, preferences, and campus relevance.
The app reduces the awkwardness of starting conversations by allowing users to express themselves through prompts, photos, interests, and profile details. This gives other users a better reason to start a conversation and makes the first interaction easier.
By using university email login, Contrast creates a more trusted environment where students know the platform is focused on campus-based users.
The admin panel further supports the platform by allowing authorized users to monitor users, manage communities, review activity, and maintain better control over the application.

12. System Workflow
The overall workflow of Contrast is as follows:
1.	A student signs up or logs in using their university email.
2.	The system verifies the university-based access.
3.	The student completes their profile by adding photos, prompts, interests, hometown, language, and preferences.
4.	The application shows relevant student profiles for discovery.
5.	The student can send likes or express interest.
6.	When two users show mutual interest, a match is created.
7.	Matched users can start chatting inside the application.
8.	Students can also explore and participate in interest-based communities.
9.	Admin users manage users, communities, activity records, reports, and app settings through the admin panel.
This workflow creates a trusted and structured way for students to meet, match, chat, and build campus connections.

Conclusion
Contrast is a social connection application designed for university students. It helps students discover people on campus, express interest, match, chat, and join communities in a more structured and trusted environment.
The application solves the discovery gap, conversation gap, trust gap, community gap, and profile-building gap that students often face in campus life.
By combining a mobile application, backend system, database, admin panel, university email verification, matchmaking, chat, prompts, photos, and communities, Contrast provides a complete platform for building meaningful campus connections in a simple and intentional way.
